## 1. Where 2.1 is built: the `hoenn-2.1` branch (default)
- **2.1 has its own integration branch, `hoenn-2.1`**, cut from `hoenn` at the 2.1 plan's merge (`16c0815317`, PR #103). `hoenn` stays pure 2.0 for Phase G (38 sweep, 39 pre-pass, 40 Bryan's playtest).
- Part branches: `v2.1-NN-<slug>` off `hoenn-2.1`; PRs go **into `hoenn-2.1`**, never into `hoenn` or `master`.
- **Syncing with 2.0:** whoever merges a 2.0 fix into `hoenn` also merges `hoenn` into `hoenn-2.1` (a merge, never a rebase or force push), then runs `tools/hoenn/regen_all.sh` and rebuilds. When 2.0 ships, `hoenn-2.1` merges into `hoenn` once, as 2.1's release PR.
- *Bryan can change this:* the other options were building 2.1 straight into `hoenn`, or not starting until 2.0 ships.

## 2. Who is who
- **Lane B builder:** the account that reviewed 2.0 (this file's author). It builds lane B's parts back to back.
- **Lane A builder:** another account (2.0's lane A builder unless Bryan says otherwise).
- **Reviewer:** another account, which reviews and merges every builder PR. **Builders never merge their own PRs** (2.0's part 34b/37 self-merges are not a pattern to repeat). Review notes go in `hoenn-docs/2.1-review-notes.md`, written only by the reviewer.

## 5. Rules carried over from 2.0 (`README.md` section 3), with 2.1's names
1. **Maps:** a lane owns the maps its parts touch. Touching a map the other lane has an open part on needs a line in your lane status file first.
2. **Shared code:** lane A's 2.1 code goes in `src/hoenn_2_1_lane_a.c` + `data/scripts/hoenn_2_1_lane_a.inc`, lane B's in `..._lane_b...` (or a part's own file, e.g. `hoenn_journal.c`). 2.1-02 creates them and chains the phone (`HoennLaneBDueCall` → `Hoenn21LaneADueCall` → `Hoenn21LaneBDueCall`).
3. **Generated files: never hand-merge.** Take the target branch's side, keep both sides of the inputs, run `regen_all.sh`.
4. **Generated IDs never move:** any generator that hands out flags (the journal's) appends in a stable order and never reuses a deleted entry's slot.
5. **Claim / sync / merge:** claim by pushing the branch and a line in your status file; merge `hoenn-2.1` into your branch before marking the PR ready; the reviewer merges one at a time; builders don't wait for a merge to start the next part.
6. **Docs:** each lane keeps status in its own file (`2.1-lane-a.md`, `2.1-lane-b.md`), size rows in its own CLAUDE.md table ("2.1 lane A/B sizes"), notes in its own CLAUDE.md section, playtest items in its own checklist section.
7. **Budget:** 2.1 as a whole is planned at 200–250 KB (plan section 19); a part over 25 KB says why.
